# Vlog

A personal, upbeat diary of the trip: feels like the traveler talking you through their day.

These are directions, not limits. Break any of them when the footage gives you a better idea.

- **Pacing:** lively, 3–8 s per shot, with the clips' own sound when they have it; jump cuts inside a clip are fine and on style.
- **Transitions:** mostly straight cuts and jump cuts; a quick `slideleft`/`slideright` or `coverleft` between places, `zoomin` into a highlight, `circleopen` for a playful reveal, a fast `fadewhite` for a new day.
- **Motion:** fast-forward (2–4×) through travel or walking bits, slow motion on a fun moment, punch-in zooms for emphasis, a freeze frame with a label, a picture-in-picture or side-by-side for two things happening.
- **Look:** bright and warm, slightly lifted saturation, natural skin tones.
- **Text:** friendly and playful. Rounded or handwritten-feel fonts (rounded system font, Avenir Next Demi Bold, Chalkboard SE, Marker Felt, Bradley Hand) in white with a coloured box or outline, slightly rotated stickers, emoji-free. Day stamps, place labels, captions about what is happening on screen, a title that pops in over the first clip.
- **Music:** light and cheerful lo-fi or indie pop. 85–110 bpm, a swung or laid-back beat (soft kick, snare or clap, shuffled hats), electric piano or plucked chords, a sub or round bass, a simple whistle-like lead. It sits under the clips' own sound: duck it (sidechain or volume) whenever there is talking or a sound worth hearing, and bring it up between them.
