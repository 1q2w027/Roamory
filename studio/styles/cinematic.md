# Cinematic

A short film of the trip, cut like a trailer for an epic: big, sweeping and dramatic. The viewer should feel the scale of the place and a story building to a climax.

These are directions, not limits. Break any of them when the footage gives you a better idea.

- **Pacing:** a three-act build. Open slow and ominous (one long held shot, 5–8 s), tighten through the middle (2–4 s per shot, accelerating), hit a climax of rapid cuts on the biggest moments, then one long final shot after a beat of silence. Hold the best shot back for the climax.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on hits and `fadeblack` smash-to-black between acts are home base; a cut to black with a beat of silence before a reveal; `fadewhite` flashes on impacts; `dissolve` or `fadeslow` only for the opening and the final shot. Contrast is the drama: long holds against sudden cuts.
- **Motion:** slow push-ins on the opening and on faces (10–20 % over the shot); slow motion at the peak of movement (a wave breaking, a jump, a crowd turning); speed ramps that slam from fast into slow on a hit; a short freeze on the climax frame.
- **Look:** letterbox bars (2.39:1 inside the 16:9 frame) throughout, a bold teal-and-orange grade with crushed blacks and deep contrast, a heavy vignette, visible film grain. Push silhouettes, sun flares and skies.
- **Text:** epic and spare. Large, wide-tracked serif or condensed caps (Didot, Bodoni 72, Baskerville, Avenir Next Condensed, Helvetica Neue Condensed Bold). Trailer-style title cards on black between acts, a few words each, cut in on a hit; the title slams in at the climax or rises slowly out of black at the end.
- **Music:** an epic trailer score. 70–100 bpm, a minor key (D minor, C minor), low drones and a sparse piano or cello motif to open, ostinato strings building through the middle, taiko-style drums and booming low hits, braams on the act breaks, a long riser into a full-orchestra climax on the biggest shot, a hard stop to silence, then the motif alone to close. Original sound only where it lands hard (a crash of surf, a shout, a bell), cut in during a silence.
