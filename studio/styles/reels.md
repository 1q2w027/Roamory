# Reels

A vertical, high-energy edit for social: punchy, rhythmic, made to stop the scroll in the first second.

These are directions, not limits. Break any of them when the footage gives you a better idea.

- **Pacing:** fast, 1.5–3 s per shot, cut to the beat (every 1 or 2 beats). Open on the most striking moment, not the first.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the beat are the base; mix in whip pans (`slideleft`/`slideup` at 0.15–0.25 s), `zoomin`, quick flashes (`fadewhite` at 0.1–0.2 s), `pixelize` or `squeezeh` for a glitch, and match cuts on movement direction.
- **Motion:** punch-in zooms on the beat (scale 1.0 → 1.15 over a few frames), speed ramps (fast into slow), a short freeze frame on a peak, a camera shake on a drop, a split screen or a quick 2×2 grid of moments.
- **Look:** punchy contrast and saturation, crisp sharpening; a flash or colour pop on drops.
- **Text:** big, bold and short. Heavy or black weights, uppercase, tight tracking (Futura, Impact, Avenir Next Heavy, DIN Condensed, rounded). Kinetic: words that pop in on beats (scale overshoot), slide in from the side, or appear one word at a time; a hook line at the start ("Kyoto in 30 seconds", "Japan, day one"), short labels on a few shots, a bold end card. Keep text in the middle 80 % of the frame, away from the top and bottom edges where social apps put their buttons.
- **Music:** upbeat and loud. 110–128 bpm, a four-on-the-floor or trap-like kick, claps or snares on 2 and 4, hats on the off-beats, a bass line, a short catchy lead or pluck hook, a riser and drop timed to the best shot. Start the music at full energy (or with a 1–2 bar build), and end on a hit at the last frame.
